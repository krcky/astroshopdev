## 1. Astrodienst (astro.com) shop: which reports exist, prices, length, forecast period, delivery

### Takeaway
Astrodienst sells a small line of long, author-branded reports in two price tiers. Robert Pelletier's Personal Portrait is about 26.90 (USD/EUR) for 25–35 pages. Liz Greene and Robert Hand reports cost about EUR 61.90–67.90 / USD 69.90–74.90 for 18–50 pages. Each comes as an "E-Horoscope" PDF (download access for 6 months) or as a printed, bound copy mailed only within the EU, Switzerland and the UK. Forecast reports cover any 12-month period that starts on the first day of a month the buyer chooses. Free "short/try-out" editions of the same reports act as the sales funnel.

### Inferences
- Astrodienst sells the author's name rather than the software. Hand's and Greene's reports cost about 2.5× the Pelletier Portrait while being similar in length. For Astro Shop this supports branding paid PDFs with the astrologer's name and photo (Boban Vujović), which matches CLAUDE.md rule 7: the corpus is the moat.
- The forecast period is chosen by the buyer ("first day of any month"), not fixed to a calendar year. Astro Shop could sell "Moja 2027" as a fixed January–December product (simple to market at New Year) and also offer "the next 12 months from a month you choose" (a better fit for birthdays and gifting).
- The extra partner copy at 8.90 is a low-friction upsell that fits a sign-pair or synastry product.
- The 6-month download limit plus "save or print it" suggests Astrodienst does not want to host PDFs forever. Astro Shop could keep purchased reports permanently in the app as a differentiator.

### Gaps

## 2. Other report sellers and white-label report engines: prices and formats

### Takeaway
The market has three price tiers for automated reports. At the bottom, Cafe Astrology sells very long PDFs for USD 3.50–7.50. In the middle, astrology.com, Astrosofa, Astromitra and Astrotheme charge USD 19.95–38 (GBP 19.90, EUR 19.99) for 12-month forecasts, solar returns and partner reports. At the top, Astrodienst's author reports cost about EUR 62–68. Human-written readings start around USD 49. White-label engines that generate branded PDFs exist: DivineAPI, AstrologyAPI, Solar Fire/Astrolabe and AstrolDeluxe ReportWriter, with one-time licences of about USD 99–360.

- Astro Shop does not need a white-label engine: it already has the ephemeris and the corpus. The engines show what competitors' reports are built from, namely cookbook text blocks per aspect.

## 5. Gift mechanics: buying for someone else, gift cards, delivery, timing; evidence on gifting share

### Takeaway
Sellers handle gifting the same way everywhere: the buyer enters the recipient's birth data at checkout, the report goes by email as a PDF (or is mailed in print), and sometimes the recipient's name goes on the cover. Astrodienst has a dedicated "The ideal Present" page and warns that orders before the holidays take longer to ship. No source gave a quantitative share of gift sales; the evidence that gifting matters is qualitative.

### Cited Findings
- **Astrodienst gifting.** A dedicated page frames reports as presents: they can be re-read over the years, and printed versions suit family members or close friends. Shipping before the holidays usually takes longer, so order in good time. Printing is done by international partners, so covers vary by country while the content stays the same. Printed copies ship to the EU, Switzerland and the UK only (shipping to the USA was possible until mid-March 2024). Recipients elsewhere are advised to take the online PDF and print it. Orders cannot be cancelled. A missing printed order is resent after three weeks — [astro.com The ideal Present](https://www.astro.com/prod/pr_spoc_e.htm); [astro.com shop FAQ](https://www.astro.com/cgi/xa.cgi/faq/fq_sh_order_e.htm)

- **Etsy.** AstraRead lets buyers order "for someone else" with that person's date, time and place of birth, delivers a PDF through Etsy messages, and gives no refunds on personalized items. ## 6. Which report types buyers value most, and what makes a computer report feel personal

### Takeaway
Evidence on perceived value is thin and mostly indirect. What can be seen: buyers pay much more for an author's name (astro.com) and for human or "no sugar coating" readings (Etsy) than for long automated text. The main criticism of computer reports is "cookbook" interpretation: each factor read in isolation, producing contradictions and duplicated template text. Sellers make products feel personal with names on covers, covers chosen by sign, proofs and previews before purchase, exact dates and timelines, and partner copies.

### Cited Findings
- **The "cookbook astrology" critique.** A professional astrologer defends computer reports (most are "outstanding and written by professional astrologers with years of experience") but warns that each influence is interpreted "as a stand alone entity without considering mitigating factors from others", which can produce apparent contradictions — [Marcha Fox, Definitive guide to astrological reports](https://bublish.com/bubble/stream/marchafox/the-definitive-guide-to-astrological-reports-2639)
- A skeptics' article (Sept 2024) compared reports from several programs to test congruence and the formulas behind them — [NZ Skeptics, "The fault in our stars: digitising astrology"](https://articles.skeptics.nz/2024/09/16/the-fault-in-our-stars-digitising-astrology)
- Competitors explain price gaps by who writes the interpretation. Cheap computer reports are described as "assembled from pre-written text blocks" and "not personalized synthesis" — [my-zodiac-ai synastry cost](https://my-zodiac-ai.com/readings/how-much-does-a-synastry-reading-cost.md); [my-zodiac-ai solar return comparison](https://my-zodiac-ai.com/readings/best-solar-return-reports-2026.md) (biased, competitor-written)
- A review of free Astrodienst reports found them "a mixed bag" whose "interpretive depth varied considerably" — [n8n.thndr.app write-up](https://n8n.thndr.app/newszone/astrodienst-free-%e2%80%93-what-we-found-will-surprise-you) (low-quality site; weak evidence)
- Personalization signals sellers use:
  - recipient's name printed on the cover and cover picked by Sun sign — [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1549172712/personalized-astrology-birth-chart-book)
  - proof sent before printing — [Etsy shop StarsLifeLab](https://www.etsy.com/shop/StarsLifeLab)
  - full preview of all pages — [Wonderbly](https://wonderbly.com/personalized-products/personalised-astrology-magical-edition-book)
  - "Advanced Integration" section plus a separate personal guidance reading — [Etsy listing 4295226687](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4295226687)
  - "no sugar coating" positioning — [Etsy astrology reading market](https://www.etsy.com/market/astrology_reading)
  - a date-by-date timeline (Cafe Astrology "Time Line" 175+ pages) — [Cafe Astrology Time Line](https://cafeastrology.com/shop/product/time-line-forecast-report/)
- Sellers increasingly state whether a reading is human-written or AI-generated — [search summary of Etsy personalized birth chart book market](https://www.etsy.com/market/personalized_birth_chart_book)
- An author's name carries price. Astrodienst prices Hand and Greene reports (25–50 pages) at EUR 61.90–67.90, against 26.90 for the 25–35 page Pelletier Portrait — [astro.com Transits of the Year](https://www.astro.com/prod/pr_tj_e.htm); [astro.com Personal Portrait](https://www.astro.com/prod/pr_ap_z.htm)

### Inferences
- For Astro Shop, the features that would make a computer report feel personal map onto assets it already has:
  - the astrologer's own voice and name (corpus by Boban Vujović)
  - exact dates: when each transit starts, peaks and ends, computed by `trajanjeTranzita`
  - ranking by importance (`lib/tvoj-dan.ts` / `oblasti.ts` scoring) instead of listing every aspect
  - month-by-month life-area scores (krug života, 8 areas)
  - the person's own natal wheel and the year's sky drawn in the app's style
  - the recipient's name and Sun-sign cover (Sun-sign story art exists)
  - These address the "cookbook" critique by prioritising and dating events rather than listing every factor.
- A free preview, as astro.com does with its "Try-Out Edition" and Short Report Forecast, fits Astro Shop rule 18c ("free = same screens, less depth"). For example: show January and the three biggest transits of "Moja 2027" for free, and lock the rest.
